# Méthodos Specification

**Identifier:** MTH-018

**Title:** Role

**Status:** Draft

## Abstract

A Role defines a set of responsibilities assumed by a Participant.

Roles define responsibility, not identity or implementation.

## Principles

- Roles are temporary.
- A Participant may assume multiple Roles.
- A Role may be performed by multiple Participants.

## Core Roles

- Principal
- Orchestrator
- Executor
- Reviewer
- Observer

## Normative Requirements

- MTH-018-REQ-001: Roles SHALL define responsibilities.
- MTH-018-REQ-002: Roles SHALL NOT define Capabilities.
- MTH-018-REQ-003: Roles SHALL be assignable to Participants.
